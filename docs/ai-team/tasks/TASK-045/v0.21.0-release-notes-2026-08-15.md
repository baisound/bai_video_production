# BAI Video Production v0.21.0

`v0.21.0` is a backward-compatible Product release that promotes the integrated
post-v0.20.1 development line after TASK-045 compatibility and native acceptance.

## Implemented

- versioned Product Projects with coordinated save/recovery, compensating
  history, Autosave, verified Backup/restore and durable Product Jobs;
- user-facing Production Control, Audit, Planning, generation-safety,
  Continuity, Prompt Evidence, Generation Queue and Audio Workspace foundations;
- frame-authoritative Timeline Audio plus practical bounded Timeline projection,
  exact editing history and per-job Export Queue recovery;
- explicit no-manifest legacy discovery, code-registered lossless copy-on-write
  migration with backup/reopen proof, and bounded Asset keyset paging.

## Native-validated

- the final Windows one-dir candidate opened and reopened an owned synthetic
  v0.20.1 Project without rewriting its Manifest;
- semantic Windows UI Automation/Narrator controls, three current displays,
  Timeline zoom/scroll interaction, native picker cancellation and
  conversation-free same-profile restart passed;
- wheel clean install, two-hour/10,000-clip acceptance and full Windows/WSL2
  regression passed in the recorded TASK-045 environments.

These claims do not extend to every machine or to arbitrary old/corrupt Project
formats. Only explicitly supported, tested formats are covered.

## Hosted-validated

P-RC-1 and P-RC-2 passed their GitHub Actions matrices and merged through PRs
#75 and #76. The release PR, exact merge SHA, annotated Tag, Release workflow and
published asset hashes are recorded in the final TASK-045 Release Evidence.

## Parked and not claimed

- TASK-013 Native H3 completion and automatic replay of its prior attempts;
- paid TASK-014 narration or any other paid Provider execution;
- automatic Candidate acceptance, Human GO, destructive or ambiguous migration;
- Production Deploy;
- general completion of all later Product roadmap work.

## Planned

Further TASK-027 orchestration, a separately reviewed safe TASK-013 Native H3
route, later TASK-014/TASK-026/TASK-041 audio work and other unreached roadmap
items remain future governed work. Listing them here grants no implementation,
paid execution, native replay, Release or Deploy authority.

## Upgrade and migration

v0.20.1 Projects remain readable. A supported migration is previewed, backed up,
applied copy-on-write and reopened before success. Unknown, newer, corrupt, lossy
or ambiguous formats fail closed. Legacy Projects without a Manifest require an
explicit Project identity and are never silently adopted.
